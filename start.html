Common worries

Sound familiar?

"I'm not flexible enough."

You don't need flexibility to start Pilates. Pilates gives you flexibility. Everyone starts somewhere, and that somewhere is exactly where you are right now.

"I don't have any equipment."

Just a thick Pilates mat. That's genuinely it. No reformer, no weights, no studio membership. Your living room floor is the only studio you need.

"I'm too old — or too out of shape."

Pilates is designed for every body. The movements are controlled and low impact — perfect for any age or fitness level. That's not a disclaimer, it's the point.

"I won't stick to it."

Three sessions a week, thirty minutes each, on your schedule, from your living room. The barrier really is as low as it gets. You don't even have to find your keys.

"I've tried before and given up."

You didn't have the right structure. A week-by-week programme that builds gradually is a completely different experience to random YouTube videos. This was designed to keep you going.

"What if I'm doing it wrong?"

Caroline coaches every movement in detail. You'll know exactly what you should be feeling in your body, and why — so you're never just going through the motions.
