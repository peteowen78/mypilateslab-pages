The challenge

Your team is working harder.
But are they moving enough?

Desk work takes a measurable toll

511,000 workers in Great Britain were suffering from a work-related musculoskeletal disorder last year. Of those cases, 84% affected the back, neck or upper limbs — exactly what sitting all day loads hardest.HSE, 2024/25

Traditional perks go unused

Gym memberships, cycle-to-work schemes, office fruit — great in theory. But when your team works remotely, perks that need travel don't get used. This one works from the sofa.

It adds up to real lost time

Musculoskeletal disorders caused 7.1 million lost working days — 27% of all work-related ill health, and a fifth of every day lost to it. Across the UK, 148.8 million days went to sickness in 2025.HSE, 2024/25 · ONS, 2025

Figures from HSE, Work-related musculoskeletal disorders in Great Britain 2025 (data to March 2025) and ONS, Sickness absence in the UK labour market 2025. They describe the scale of the problem, not outcomes from this programme.
