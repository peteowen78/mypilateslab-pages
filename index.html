About your instructor

Hello,
I'm Caroline.

Pilates for everyBODY.

As founder of My Pilates Lab, my main aim is to make Pilates accessible to everyBODY. I want you to fall in love with how your body feels and embed this wonderful form of movement into your lifestyle — whatever your age or ability.

Following two decades as a subject lead in GCSE and A Level Dance and Performing Arts, I've been a practising Level 4 Pilates instructor for five years — helping people alleviate aches and pains, keep daily niggles at bay, and keep doing what they love.

My Pilates Lab is not about me. You won't see insta stories full of selfies or endless curated self-promotion. But rest assured I'll beaver away behind the scenes to curate classes that keep you healthy and strong.
